Star lab

A reimplementation of JoTrdl's Star Fluid Experiment: 262,144 particles riding a single-pass compressible fluid solver, drawn additively as gold points over a cool dye field. Three lobes orbit and feed it on their own; drag to add your own.

Presets

Air, particles and colour only. The orbit driver is left as you set it.

The fluid

Stiffness stands in for pressure: no iterative solve, one algebraic term pushing velocity away from wherever the density-like channel has piled up.

Particles

262,144 particles, one texel each in a 512x512 position/age texture. Refilled through a ring buffer, one full row per emission, using the same mechanism the source uses.

Autoplay

Not in the source, which only ever emits on a mouse drag. Added so the page has something to look at with nobody at the pointer.

Colour

Defaults match the source exactly: dye (0.3, 0.5, 1.1), particles (1.0, 0.5, 0.166) at 0.66 alpha, drawn additively.

The rig

A solid obstacle dropped into the orbit, off by default — the source this lab reimplements never had one. The same jib-and-main section every other lab sails with; only the sails are solid to the fluid, the hull is drawn but not.
