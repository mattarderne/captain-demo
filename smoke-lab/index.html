The air
The smoke

Indigo is dead air, teal is the free stream, yellow and white are the fast stuff. Watch the colour lift as the flow squeezes through the slot.

Particles

Dust: one pixel per particle, lagging the flow by the drag time constant, blown out additively where they bunch. Trails: each particle carries an age, fades in and out over its life, and paints into a fading streak buffer. Both respect the sails as solid and wrap at the domain edge.

The rig

Below, fore / aft and athwartships are where each sail is attached: the jib at the stemhead, the main at the mast, in her own lengths. Each sail pivots about that point, so angle sheets the leech in and out while the luff stays put. The slot follows from where the mast is and how hard each is sheeted.
